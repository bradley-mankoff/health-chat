# Disclaimer — Not Medical Advice

**This software is for information only. It is not medical advice, not a medical device, and not a substitute for professional medical judgment.**

- It summarizes information found in **your own records** and in **general medical reference material** (guidelines, test directories). It does not diagnose, prognose, or recommend treatment.
- Lab reference ranges vary by lab, assay, age, sex, and clinical context. A value flagged “High” or “Low” in one report may be normal in another context. Always confirm with the lab report and your clinician.
- The local language model can make mistakes even when grounded in retrieved text. Verify any important claim against the cited source and your original report.
- **If you have an urgent medical concern, contact your clinician, urgent care, or emergency services — do not rely on this tool.**
